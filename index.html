About Me

I'm a full-stack software engineer in the Twin Cities. Since January 2024 I've worked at Level2, a digital health company, where I build event-driven systems on AWS that handle sensitive member health data: TypeScript and .NET services, DynamoDB, SNS/SQS pipelines and React front ends. I also led the team's adoption of AI-assisted development with tools like Claude Code and GitHub Copilot.

I graduated from the University of St. Thomas with a B.S. in Computer Science in December 2023. I'm most motivated by work that helps people, especially in health, civic tech and nonprofit spaces. Outside of work I volunteer as a hospice companion and as a tech coach for older adults, and I like to lift weights, run and go hiking.
